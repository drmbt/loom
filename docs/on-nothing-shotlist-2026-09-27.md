# "ON NOTHING" shot list (2026-09-27)

This is the cut list of the reference, `renders/on-nothing/reference/ref.mp4` (1920×818, 23.976 fps,
2:10), written so that every shot has an owner or is plainly marked UNASSIGNED. The look brief is in
[on-nothing-look-reference-2026-09-27.md](on-nothing-look-reference-2026-09-27.md) and the build plan
is in [on-nothing-shots-plan-2026-09-27.md](on-nothing-shots-plan-2026-09-27.md).

## How it was cut

- The cuts were detected with `ffmpeg -vf "select='gt(scene,0.25)',showinfo"`. That found 164 cuts,
  or 165 segments. Each segment's middle frame was then checked by eye on contact sheets, and every
  segment of 0.9 s or longer was checked at its start, middle and end.
- The detector misses some cuts inside long, dark or smeared segments. Five were found by eye and
  are listed with a "~" time below. It also splits whips, flash frames and one-frame strobes
  (0.04–0.2 s) into segments of their own. These are folded into the shot they belong to, and a run
  of strobe cuts on one subject is listed as one row. The list therefore has **110 shots (rows)**,
  not the detector's 165 segments. **71 of them are UNASSIGNED**, counting the rows that only fall
  inside another owner's time window.
- Framing and lens are read from the frames: "wide" means about 18–24 mm, "normal" about 35–50 mm
  and "macro/tele" means a very thin focus plane. Almost every shot is handheld with some roll. The
  exceptions are the locked, symmetrical tableaux.
- Owners are from the 2026-09-27 assignment: **title** (0:00–0:02, plus the ring-light silhouette
  ~0:02–0:04), **tableau** (0:06–0:20, the lead), **zoom** (~0:22–0:26), **split** (~0:27–0:29),
  **prism** (~0:28–0:31), **halo** (~0:39–0:42), **crt** (~0:50–0:53), **cyc** (~1:14–1:20 and
  1:43–1:47), **mirror** (~1:22–1:25), **quad** (~1:47–1:51), **wheel** (~1:58–2:05) and
  **closeups** (this row: the UNASSIGNED shots built so far). A shot that only falls inside an
  owner's time window, but is a different kind of shot, is marked "(window)".

Times are seconds (and m:ss).

## UNASSIGNED shots, by kind

71 rows are UNASSIGNED. They come in six kinds, and each kind recurs through the video:

1. **Performer MCU/CU with sunglasses and beanie, low angle** (rows 9, 24, 30–33, 39, 41, 44, 51, 55,
   58, 64, 68, 83, 86, 88–89, 93–94): the most common shot in the video. The face is almost always
   dark, rim-lit from behind, with the tubes or the headlights smeared into columns behind.
   The ECU of row 24 was tried on the MPFB head (closeups, 2026-09-27) and dropped: at 50 cm the
   head reads as a mannequin, with no pores or eye detail. It needs a scanned-quality head asset.
2. **Hands with rings / iced wrists** (rows 3, 47, 57, 59, 62): tele CU, handheld jolts.
3. **Jewellery macro** (rows 4, 48, 60, 90; the lead's 15 is the same set-up): the pendant or the
   chain on a paper-thin focus plane, with stone bokeh. **Built: `pendant` (closeups).**
4. **Sneaker** (rows 5–7, 95): the white low-top held up, pushed into the lens, or set on a black
   bonnet in front of the car row. **Built: `sneaker`, row 95 (closeups).**
5. **Car details** (rows 28, 29, 106): a fender, a 3/4 front with streaks, a wheel.
6. **Wide warehouse coverage** of the figure between the cars (rows 10, 38, 40, 43, 63, 69, 82, 87,
   92): these are the tableau's set with other cameras.

The two title cards (rows 108–109) are graphics, not renders.
