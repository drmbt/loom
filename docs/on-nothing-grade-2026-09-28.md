# On Nothing: the grade, measured (2026-09-28)

T1407b (grade). The owner's verdict on the rebuilt first minute (rows 1-62, 125 parts) was that ours is cleaner and more contrasty than the reference. The reference is hazier and more washed: lifted grey blacks, lower contrast, more lens bloom and halation, near-grey skin, and murkier cars where only the lamps and bumpers catch. This document measures that gap part by part. It then describes the one shared finish built to close it (`src/projects/on-nothing/finish.ts`) and how well it did.

## How it is measured

`measure.py` (`renders/on-nothing/agents/grade/tools/`) reads the reference and ours frame-aligned. Ours is the assembled cut `cuts/full-60final.mp4` (before) or each part's own row clip (drafts, finals). It scores every frame of every part and takes the median over the part's frames. All values are **8-bit display levels (0-255) of the decoded video**, luma Rec.709, as a viewer sees them:

| measure | what it is |
|---|---|
| p2, p10, p50, p90, p98 | luma percentiles over a 480-wide block mean of the frame. **p2 is the black level.** |
| contrast | p90 − p10 |
| chroma, Cb, Cr by band | mean (max−min) chroma and mean Cb/Cr offsets in three tone bands: shadows (luma 6-45), mids (45-120), highlights (120+). Cb > 0 is blue, Cr > 0 is red: **cyan is Cb+ Cr−, sodium orange is Cb− Cr+** |
| warm %, cyan % | share of lit, coloured pixels (luma ≥ 12, chroma ≥ 10) that are orange or cyan |
| glow 8-24 px, glow 24-56 px | mean luma of a ring 8-24 px (then 24-56 px, at 1920 wide) around the hot highlights (luma ≥ 200 and ≥ p99.7), above the frame median, as a share of the highlights' own level above it. This is the halation and bloom measure. |
| grain fine | median std of a 5×5 high-pass in flat 32 px tiles of shadow-to-mid level, at 1920 |
| grain coarse | the same on the 480-wide picture (texture 4-20 px) |

The flash parts are excluded from the global medians because their `post` curves lift the whole frame (1a, 17a, 25a, 26b/d/f, 34b, 39b/d/f, 53b, 59b/d, 62a/c). So are the white-cyc prism parts (26a/c/e/g), which are a separate world.

What that says:

- **The blacks are the gap.** The reference never goes below about 7 (p2 7, p10 9). Its dark rooms sit at 13-15, not 0. Ours sits at 0-1 almost everywhere. This is the "lifted grey blacks" and most of the "more contrasty" the owner sees. By p90 − p10 ours is actually *lower* in contrast. The black floor at 0 is what reads as hard.
- **Ours is about a stop darker in the mids** (p50 2.5 against 23, p90 74 against 127). Part of that is content: the reference's rooms show dim structure where ours are empty void. Part of it is the MCUs (see per-part), whose faces are much darker than the reference's.
- **The whites are lower in the reference.** It tops out around 245 and never clips; ours reaches 253-255 on the lamps and flash parts.
- **Colour:** our shadows are warmer and twice as saturated as the reference's near-neutral, faintly cyan shadows. The reference's mids lean teal (Cb+, Cr−), ours are neutral. Its highlights are less blue than ours: a green-yellow white, where ours is cold blue-white. There is far more cyan in the reference (3.7 % against 0.8 %) and a little less orange.
- **Glow:** the near ring (8-24 px) matches. The wide one (24-56 px) is half again stronger in the reference: a wide veil around every light, not a tighter bloom. The ring is not red, so there is no film halation tint to add.
- **Grain:** the reference as delivered shows almost none (0.3 levels at both scales; the web encode ate it). Ours shows 2.4 fine and 1.6 coarse. Ours is not cleaner in grain; it is several times grainier. What reads as "clean" is the crisp black floor and the absent veil.

## The finish

`finish.ts` holds `withFinish(document)`. `onNothingDocument` applies it once to every builder's result, so every shot gets it: the dispatch now builds the shot with `shotDocument` and wraps it. A plate built by `asPlate` (`plate: true`) is returned unfinished, because the shot that composites it is finished as a whole. `withFinish` refuses a document that already has a finish. It inserts, between the shot's last pass and `out`:

```
frame ─┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       └─ finishDown (Custom WGSL, fixed 480×204) ─ finishNear (Blur 4) ─ finishWide (Blur 24)
                                                         │                     │
                                     finish (Custom WGSL · Multi) ─────────────┴─ out
```

- **Why not Film Grade:** the stock Film Grade always applies its Hable curve. After the shot grades (which already tone map) it would tone map twice, and it has no identity setting. `halo` is a ring flare and `lens` is distortion and vignette; neither is a glow. The glow is two stock **Blur**s at a fixed 480×204 size, so it reaches the same share of the frame at a 960 draft and a 3840 `--final`. The rest is one Custom WGSL pass.
- **Mist (halation and haze veil), in linear light:** `m = lin·(1 − mist − mistWide) + near·mist + wide·mistWide`. `mistWide` 0.02 on the wide blur (~100 px at 1920) is the veil the 24-56 px ring asked for. The near `mist` is 0: the toe alone raised the 8-24 px ring above the reference's.
- **Curve, in display level:** toe (a gamma), contrast about a pivot, then black lands at `lift` and white at `white`. Display level here is **exactly the sRGB value the Output writes**. The first cut used a 2.2 power instead, and a 0.022 lift came out at level 1 of 255, not 5.6: the two curves part ways near black.
- **Tone-banded tint:** a small RGB offset added to the shadows, mids and highlights (luma-weighted bands), which leaves luma unchanged. Then **saturation**: one value for the mids and highlights, one for the shadows.
- **Grain:** the finish owns the film's grain. Every other grain in the graph (every shot grade's own `grain`) is set to 0, and the finish carries one grain with the grade's formula (`grain`, `grainSize`). render.ts therefore lifts it out and adds it after the accumulation under `--final` (T1432b), so it survives `--final` exactly as the grades' grain did.

### The global setting, and where it came from

| knob | value | from |
|---|---|---|
| lift | 0.023 | black p2 → 8-9 once the contrast, the grain and the H.264 encode have had their say (the encode alone takes ~1 level off the blacks) |
| toe | 0.8 | the dark voids 0-2 → 10-18 (the reference's dark rooms 13-15). A quantile fit over all 110 parts wanted **0.55**, with p50 23 and p90 127 matched. On a render, that brightened every car body and streak, which is the opposite of the owner's "murkier cars", so the mids go only part of the way. |
| contrast / pivot | 0.95 / 0.45 | the owner's "lower contrast": it also raises the floor |
| white | 0.9 | the reference never clips; its p98 highlights sit under 245 |
| mistWide / wide | 0.02 / Blur 24 | glow 24-56 px 0.17 → 0.24-0.27 (reference 0.26) |
| mist / near | 0 / Blur 4 | the near ring was already at 0.40-0.45 against the reference's 0.35 |
| saturation / shadowSaturation | 1.45 / 1.5 | band chroma after the lift (the lifted neutral floor dilutes the shadows' chroma) |
| shadowTint, midTint, highlightTint | (−0.005, +0.0012, +0.0032), (−0.0111, +0.0026, +0.0074), (−0.0065, +0.0034, −0.0147) | the band (Cb, Cr) offsets, fitted by fixed-point iteration on an emulation of the finish over 220 frames (shadows and mids toward cyan, highlights toward a green-yellow white) |
| grain / grainSize | 0.006 / 4 | one soft grain, a fifth of the shot grades' 0.03 at 1.3 px. The reference's delivered texture is 0.3 levels; ours without any grain already measures ~1.4 coarse from the render itself. |

No per-shot overrides were added. The measurements that differ per shot (below) differ in **exposure and content**, not in the look, and a finish override would hide a lighting problem rather than fix it.
