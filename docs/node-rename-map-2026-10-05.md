# The rename map: every shipped node name, old and new

**The approved record, as of 2026-10-05.** This page and the `.json` beside it are the map as the owner and the lead approved it, with the two kinds spelled out at the sweep's step 0. They are not regenerated as the batches land: the sweep's tools rebuild what is left to rename from the shipped bytes each time, so from batch A on this page describes names that have already moved.

T1593b phase 2a, 2026-10-05. This is the list phase 2b applies.

Generated by `src/examples/rename/build-rename-map.ts` from the shipped bytes; the whole map, one row per name, is [`node-rename-map-2026-10-05.json`](node-rename-map-2026-10-05.json). Do not edit either file: change `rename-rules.ts` or `rename-judgements.ts` and run it again.

## What it covers

111 documents: every example, every starter component and the furnace and on-nothing projects. **`projects/sentinel-bot/**` and `src/projects/sentinel-bot/**` are left out**: that session renames its own names.

The gate counts 3,284 names that do not carry their kind. 31 of them are sentinel-bot's. 130 are a starter component's graph embedded again in a second and further example, which is one decision and not several. That leaves **3,123 nodes**, and this map renames all of them, plus 6 that conform already and still carry the old habit's `1` (below). 3,129 nodes, 2,142 distinct names.

A name is decided once per **scope**: an example's own graph, a component's graph wherever it is embedded, or a whole project. So the source that builds two dozen on-nothing shots can be rewritten with one answer per name.
