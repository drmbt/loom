# E78 — Aether Orrery

Five open orbital bands with beveled rectangular cross-sections and closed end faces rotate
on independent axes. Each band has a distinct radius, arc length, plane, speed, and direction.
Repeated corner vertices preserve crisp face normals; reflective PBR metal and narrow
luminous inner inserts give the bands separate body and edge detail. Real mirrored geometry
passes through E75 Resonance's colour/depth atlas and room compositor.

The centre combines a textured luminous sphere with 18 electrical filament paths made from
2,304 beam segments. The sphere and filament envelope retain their size while audio changes
their brightness. Orbital motion follows the deterministic timeline without beat-driven
translation or radial expansion.

Eighteen projection bays with alternating widths and heights, a 21-unit vault, enlarged
oculus, polished floor, helical light ribbons, and live TimeGrid video form the chamber.
Audio also changes the projection and architectural lighting.
