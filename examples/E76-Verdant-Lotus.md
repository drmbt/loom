# E76 — Verdant Lotus

A crystalline lotus suspended inside a circular projection hall derived from E75 Resonance's
full scene pipeline. Three table-driven point grids form 24 closed, eight-facet crystal
blades in outer, middle, and crown tiers. Straight facet profiles, repeated edge vertices,
and varied lengths preserve the crystal silhouette and hard face normals. The blades use
transmissive glass with green absorption, restrained dispersion, and mirrored geometry.
An upstream rendering of the room supplies the background sampled by refraction. A small
textured green core sits in the crown opening; its surface detail moves with timeline time.
Twenty-eight narrow projection bays, live TimeGrid video, depth-aware compositing, botanical
caustic curtains, haze, DOF, and bloom complete the installation. The crystal structure holds
its shape while audio changes the core intensity and room lighting.
