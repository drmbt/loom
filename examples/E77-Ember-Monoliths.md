# E77 — Ember Monoliths

Nine separately tessellated basalt slabs lean around a central aperture. Every slab
has its own point grid, point kernel, dimensions, yaw, lean, mineral variation, PBR draw, and
mirrored geometry. Twelve unusually broad projection bays make this chamber materially
different from E75 Resonance. Slow independent tectonic phases raise the bodies and shift
their upper sections across fault lines. Audio changes the fissure brightness rather than
moving the whole structure on every beat. Thin additive beams follow selected slab edges.
A reduced ceiling tier,
wide stepped plinth, rising dust columns, and a horizontal survey laser replace Resonance's
light-shaft treatment. Live panel video, depth-aware room compositing, firelight, shadows,
haze, bloom, and audio-driven fissures remain part of the graph.
